Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about orthodontic treatment and our practice

How long does orthodontic treatment take?

Treatment duration varies depending on your individual case, typically ranging from 12 to 24 months. During your free consultation, we'll provide an estimated treatment timeline based on your specific needs.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes, we accept most major insurance plans and will work with you to maximize your benefits. We also offer flexible payment plans to make treatment affordable. Learn more about our insurance and payment options.

At what age should children have their first orthodontic visit?

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends children have their first orthodontic evaluation by age 7. Early evaluation allows us to identify potential issues and determine the best time to begin treatment, if needed.
