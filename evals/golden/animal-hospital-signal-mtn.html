Your Neighborhood Animal Care Center

Providing the best care for you and your pet.

The Animal Hospital of Signal Mountain is located 10 miles outside of Chattanooga and Lookout Mountain, at the southern end of the Cumberland Plateau in the town of Signal Mountain.

Our highly-trained veterinarians & support staff provide comprehensive preventative medicine, surgical and emergency services, grooming, and boarding. We invite you to learn more about our story.
