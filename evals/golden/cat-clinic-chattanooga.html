Welcome to the Cat Clinic of Chattanooga
XC
Our entire team is dedicated to the health and well being of your cat. We know that cats are special, and cat owners are, too. We strive to respect, embrace and enhance the bond between cats and their chosen people.

Limiting our practice to cats allows us to best provide for the unique needs of your feline friends.  Every procedure and piece of equipment used in the Cat Clinic has been chosen with the special needs of cats in mind. We strive to provide a quiet, calm and supportive environment in which the healthy cat can flourish and the ill cat recover.
