Comprehensive Diesel Repair Specialists

Diesel engines require specialized expertise. From high-pressure fuel systems to turbochargers and emission components, diesel platforms demand precision service.

Blue Wrench provides professional diesel repair for:

  • Cummins diesel engines
  • Power Stroke diesel engines
  • Duramax diesel engines
  • Heavy-duty commercial trucks
  • Fleet diesel vehicles

Our diesel services include diagnostics, injector service, turbo repair, DEF system repair, brake service, suspension repair, and full diesel engine rebuilds. Whether your truck is used for towing, hauling, or commercial operation, we deliver dependable solutions built for durability.

Vehicles We Service

Blue Wrench proudly services a wide range of vehicle platforms:

Asian vehicles including Toyota, Honda, Nissan, Subaru, Mazda, Hyundai, and Kia
Domestic vehicles including Ford, Chevrolet, GMC, Ram, Dodge, and Jeep
European vehicles including BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Audi, Volkswagen, Volvo, and more
Motorhomes and RV platforms
Heavy-duty vehicles including PACCAR, Mitsubishi Fuso, and International

Each platform requires specific diagnostic procedures and repair standards. Our team applies manufacturer-informed service practices to maintain performance and safety.

Lift Kits & Suspension Upgrades

In addition to maintenance and repair, Blue Wrench provides professional lift kit installation and suspension upgrades. Whether improving ground clearance, enhancing towing stability, or upgrading appearance, our technicians ensure proper installation and alignment for long-term drivability.

We install:

  • Suspension lift kits

  • Airbag suspension systems
  • Heavy-duty shocks and springs
  • Performance suspension upgrades

Proper alignment and suspension geometry calibration ensure stable handling and even tire wear after modification.
