Complete Auto Body-Collision Repair-Refinish Specialists in Boise, ID

Owner Greg Dill incorporated in 1987 and built the permanent location on 41st street in 1998. Son Chris Dill joined as soon as he graduated from high school in 2002.

Wizard Auto Specialties is a locally owned and operated auto body repair shop in Boise, ID. We pride ourselves in doing the highest quality work and giving you the best customer service we can. We do collision repair, auto refinish, window repair, frame services, and more. We use Sikens Autowave Paint System to greatly reduce hazardous waste. We work with all insurances. We service Boise and Garden City, ID, and all surrounding areas.
