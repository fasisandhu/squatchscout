Welcome to Tech Ridge Dental
Your Austin area dentist

Drs. Sean Ma and Minnie Ma welcome you to our dental office, conveniently located near I-35 and Parmer in Austin, TX.  We see patients from Austin and all surrounding areas, including Pflugerville, Round Rock, Cedar Park, Manor, Hutto, and others.  We offer a full scope of family, restorative, and cosmetic dentistry, with additional care in preventative dental cleanings and deep cleanings, emergencies, root canals, teeth removal, bone grafting, implants, and many other dental treatments. 

Our mission is simple: provide dental care of the highest quality by the best dental team in Austin.
