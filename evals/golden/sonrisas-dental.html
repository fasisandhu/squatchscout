WHY CHOOSE SONRISAS

Specialized care, thoughtfully coordinated around you.

Choosing the right dental team means finding experienced doctors who understand your needs, coordinate every stage of care, and create a treatment plan specifically for you. At Sonrisas Dental Center — often simply called Sonrisas Dental by our patients — multiple areas of dentistry come together to make care more convenient, personal, and complete.

50+ years of combined clinical experience

Our doctors bring more than five decades of combined education, specialty training, and patient-care experience to every treatment plan.

  1. 01

    Comprehensive Care Under One Roof

    From general dentistry and orthodontics to periodontics and coordinated specialty care, our multidisciplinary team brings multiple areas of care together within one practice.

  2. 02

    Three Convenient Locations

    Receive personalized care at one of our three Central Texas offices in North Austin, Cedar Park, or South Austin.

  3. 03

    Treatment Designed for You

    Every patient is different. Our doctors customize each treatment plan around the patient's health, goals, lifestyle, development, and long-term needs.

  4. 04

    Flexible Ways to Pay

    We work with many PPO insurance plans and offer flexible payment options, including in-house and third-party financing through CareCredit and Cherry (subject to approval; terms vary).

  5. 05

    Airway-Centered Orthodontics

    Our orthodontic approach looks beyond straight teeth. We also consider breathing, sleep, facial growth, and healthy jaw development when evaluating each patient's needs.
